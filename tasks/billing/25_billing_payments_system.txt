1. OBJECTIVE STATEMENT
================================================================================

The Billing & Payments System manages the complete financial lifecycle of patient
encounters from charge capture through payment processing, reconciliation, and
reporting. This system integrates with clinical workflows to automatically generate
charges based on services provided, validates insurance coverage, processes multiple
payment methods (credit card, ACH, check), manages refunds/adjustments, and maintains
complete audit trails for healthcare compliance.

Key Responsibilities:
- Automatic charge capture from consultations, procedures, labs, imaging
- Insurance verification and eligibility checking (real-time, pre-visit, ongoing)
- Multiple payment channel support (card, ACH, check, patient payment plans)
- Balance calculation (patient responsibility, insurance portion, write-offs)
- Payment processing and reconciliation with accounting system
- Refund and adjustment workflows with approval processes
- Financial reporting for practice management
- Compliance with healthcare financial regulations (CMS, state requirements)

2. BUSINESS LOGIC & WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

2.1 BILLING WORKFLOW - AUTOMATED CHARGE CAPTURE
- Consultation completed → System auto-generates charge based on:
  * Service code (99213-99215 for office visit, 99401-99404 for preventive)
  * Provider (physician, PA, NP - different billing rates)
  * Insurance vs self-pay distinction
  * Patient age and visit type (new vs established)
  * Diagnosis codes (drives medical necessity)
  * Procedures performed during visit (adds charge codes)
  * Lab orders (generates lab charges if not included in visit)
  * Imaging orders (generates imaging charges if not included in visit)
- Auto-charge reviewed by billing staff (can override within 24 hours)
- Finance hold period (48 hours) before transmission to insurance
- Charge transmission to insurance with claim details
- Automatic posting of insurance payments and denials

2.2 INSURANCE VERIFICATION WORKFLOW
Real-time Insurance Eligibility:
- Patient registration triggers insurance lookup (eligibility, coverage details)
- Verification via insurance provider API (real-time or batch, depending on payer)
- Extract key details:
  * Insurance active/inactive status
  * Coverage type (HMO, PPO, HDHP, etc.)
  * In-network status of provider
  * Deductible (remaining balance)
  * Co-insurance percentage
  * Out-of-pocket maximum (remaining)
  * Pre-authorization requirements
- Cache verification for 30 days (can refresh on demand)
- Pre-visit alerts to patient if not covered or high out-of-pocket
- Pre-authorization submission for procedures requiring approval

2.3 CHARGE CALCULATION & BILLING BALANCE
Automatic Balance Calculation:
- Service charge: CPT code base rate × provider multiplier × location multiplier
- Deductible application: Applies deductible to self-insured portion first
- Insurance portion: (Charge - deductible) × co-insurance %
- Patient responsibility: Charge - insurance portion (before payments)
- Adjustments: Write-offs, contractual adjustments, professional courtesy
- Final patient balance = (patient responsibility + adjustments) - advance payments

Example: Office visit charge $150
- Insurance deductible: $200 remaining
- Co-insurance: 20%
- Advance payment: $30
Calculation:
- Insurance portion: $0 (deductible not met, patient pays first $150)
- Patient responsibility: $150
- Less advance payment: -$30
- Patient balance: $120

2.4 PAYMENT PROCESSING
Multiple Payment Methods:
- Credit Card: Tokenized, PCI-DSS compliant processing via Stripe/Square
- ACH: Bank account transfer (3-5 day settlement)
- Check: Manual entry, reconciliation via bank deposit
- Patient Payment Plans: Installment agreements (3, 6, 12 months) with auto-payment
- Insurance Payment: Auto-posted from ERA (electronic remittance advice)

Payment Authorization:
- Real-time card authorization (fraud detection, velocity checks)
- Failed payment retry (1-3 retries over 3 days)
- Payment decline messaging with alternative options
- Duplicate payment detection (within 5 minutes)

Payment Allocation:
- Insurance payments applied via ERA code (specific visit, general account credit)
- Patient payments applied to oldest balance first (FIFO)
- Overpayment management: store as patient credit, offer refund, apply to future
- Payment posting within 24 hours

2.5 REFUND & ADJUSTMENT WORKFLOWS
Refund Scenarios:
- Overpayment refund: Initiated by patient or practice staff
- Insurance overpayment: Refund to insurance (required by law within 30 days)
- Denied service: Patient refund if paid out-of-pocket
- Duplicate charge: Refund and charge removal

Refund Process:
- Refund request submitted with reason
- Manager approval required if amount > $100
- Refund initiated (card refund 3-5 days, check 7-10 days, ACH 1-2 days)
- Audit trail logged for compliance

Adjustments:
- Professional courtesy: Full or partial write-off (manager approval)
- Contractual adjustment: Insurance-mandated reduction (auto-applied from EOB)
- Hardship adjustment: Financial hardship write-off (manager + CFO approval)
- Bad debt write-off: After 90+ days and collection attempts (compliance tracked)

2.6 RECONCILIATION & ACCOUNTING
Daily Reconciliation:
- Batch comparison: Charges generated vs charges transmitted to insurance
- Payment reconciliation: ERA payments vs system posting
- Bank reconciliation: Bank deposits vs payment records
- Aging report: Accounts receivable aging (0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days)

Monthly Close:
- Charge write-off for uncollectible (24+ months, documented collection efforts)
- Insurance follow-up for aging claims (60+ days no response)
- Patient payment plan audits (on-track vs delinquent)
- Financial statement reporting

2.7 FINANCIAL REPORTING
Standard Reports:
- Daily deposit report: Charges, payments, adjustments, net revenue
- Aging analysis: AR aging, insurance vs patient, collectibility analysis
- Revenue recognition: Monthly revenue by provider, service line, insurance
- Provider productivity: Charges per provider, collections per provider
- Payer analysis: Top payers, denial rates, average reimbursement by payer

Compliance Reporting:
- Credit balance report (refund obligations to patients)
- 1099 reporting (independent providers paid >$600/year)
- Insurance audits: Billing practices, coding accuracy, compliance verification

3. DATA MODEL & SCHEMA
================================================================================

3.1 CORE TABLES

TABLE: charges
- charge_id (UUID, PK)
- visit_id (UUID, FK → consultations)
- provider_id (UUID, FK → providers)
- patient_id (UUID, FK → patients)
- service_code (VARCHAR, e.g., "99213") → CPT code
- service_description (VARCHAR, e.g., "Office visit, established patient")
- service_category (ENUM: "office_visit", "procedure", "lab", "imaging", "other")
- charge_amount (DECIMAL 10,2)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- created_by (UUID, FK → users)
- status (ENUM: "pending_review", "approved", "denied", "finalized")
- reviewed_at (TIMESTAMP)
- reviewed_by (UUID, FK → users)
- denial_reason (VARCHAR, nullable)
- INDEX: (visit_id, patient_id, created_at)

TABLE: patient_balances
- balance_id (UUID, PK)
- patient_id (UUID, FK → patients)
- visit_id (UUID, FK → consultations)
- charge_amount (DECIMAL 10,2)
- deductible_applied (DECIMAL 10,2)
- insurance_portion (DECIMAL 10,2)
- patient_responsibility (DECIMAL 10,2)
- adjustments_amount (DECIMAL 10,2)
- payments_received (DECIMAL 10,2)
- balance_due (DECIMAL 10,2)
- insurance_id (UUID, FK → insurance_policies)
- calculated_at (TIMESTAMP)
- updated_at (TIMESTAMP)
- INDEX: (patient_id, balance_due DESC)

TABLE: insurance_policies
- policy_id (UUID, PK)
- patient_id (UUID, FK → patients)
- insurance_company (VARCHAR, e.g., "Blue Cross Blue Shield")
- plan_name (VARCHAR)
- member_id (VARCHAR, unique per insurance)
- group_id (VARCHAR)
- deductible_amount (DECIMAL 10,2)
- deductible_remaining (DECIMAL 10,2)
- copay_amount (DECIMAL 10,2)
- coinsurance_percentage (DECIMAL 5,2, e.g., 20.00)
- out_of_pocket_max (DECIMAL 10,2)
- out_of_pocket_remaining (DECIMAL 10,2)
- is_primary (BOOLEAN)
- is_active (BOOLEAN)
- verified_at (TIMESTAMP)
- verification_expires_at (TIMESTAMP)
- INDEX: (patient_id, is_active, is_primary)

TABLE: payments
- payment_id (UUID, PK)
- patient_id (UUID, FK → patients)
- payment_method (ENUM: "credit_card", "ach", "check", "insurance")
- amount (DECIMAL 10,2)
- status (ENUM: "pending", "authorized", "captured", "failed", "refunded")
- payment_date (DATE)
- posted_date (DATE, nullable)
- reference_number (VARCHAR, unique)
- transaction_id (VARCHAR, nullable, from payment processor)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- posted_at (TIMESTAMP, nullable)
- posted_by (UUID, FK → users, nullable)
- notes (TEXT, nullable)
- INDEX: (patient_id, payment_date, status)

TABLE: payment_plans
- plan_id (UUID, PK)
- patient_id (UUID, FK → patients)
- plan_type (ENUM: "3_months", "6_months", "12_months", "custom")
- total_amount (DECIMAL 10,2)
- monthly_payment (DECIMAL 10,2)
- start_date (DATE)
- end_date (DATE)
- status (ENUM: "active", "completed", "suspended", "default")
- auto_pay_enabled (BOOLEAN)
- auto_pay_method (ENUM: "ach", "credit_card", nullable)
- payments_received (DECIMAL 10,2)
- next_payment_date (DATE)
- INDEX: (patient_id, status)

TABLE: adjustments
- adjustment_id (UUID, PK)
- patient_id (UUID, FK → patients)
- visit_id (UUID, FK → consultations)
- adjustment_type (ENUM: "professional_courtesy", "contractual", "hardship", "bad_debt")
- amount (DECIMAL 10,2)
- reason (VARCHAR)
- approval_status (ENUM: "pending", "approved", "denied")
- requested_by (UUID, FK → users)
- approved_by (UUID, FK → users, nullable)
- approved_at (TIMESTAMP, nullable)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- INDEX: (patient_id, approval_status)

TABLE: claims
- claim_id (UUID, PK)
- patient_id (UUID, FK → patients)

- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- created_by (UUID, FK → users)
- INDEX: (patient_id, created_at DESC)

4. API SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

4.1 CHARGE MANAGEMENT API

POST /api/v1/charges
Request:
{
  "visit_id": "uuid",
  "service_codes": ["99213"],  // Array of CPT codes
  "provider_id": "uuid"
}
Response:
{
  "charge_id": "uuid",
  "visit_id": "uuid",
  "service_code": "99213",
  "charge_amount": 150.00,
  "status": "pending_review",
  "created_at": "2026-01-31T10:30:00Z"
}

GET /api/v1/charges/{charge_id}
GET /api/v1/patients/{patient_id}/charges
Query params: status, date_range, service_code

PUT /api/v1/charges/{charge_id}/approve
Body: { reviewed_by: uuid }

PUT /api/v1/charges/{charge_id}/deny
Body: { denial_reason: string }

4.2 BALANCE & INSURANCE API

GET /api/v1/patients/{patient_id}/balance
Response:
{
  "charge_amount": 150.00,
  "insurance_portion": 0.00,
  "patient_responsibility": 150.00,
  "adjustments": 0.00,
  "payments_received": 0.00,
  "balance_due": 150.00
}

POST /api/v1/patients/{patient_id}/insurance/verify
Body: { policy_id: uuid }
Response: Insurance verification details with eligibility

GET /api/v1/patients/{patient_id}/insurance/policies
Response: Array of insurance policies

4.3 PAYMENT API

POST /api/v1/payments
Request:
{
  "patient_id": "uuid",
  "amount": 100.00,
  "method": "credit_card",
  "payment_method_token": "tok_visa"
}
Response:
{
  "payment_id": "uuid",
  "status": "authorized",
  "transaction_id": "ch_xxxxx"
}

GET /api/v1/patients/{patient_id}/payments
Query params: status, date_range

POST /api/v1/payments/{payment_id}/refund
Body: { amount: decimal, reason: string }

4.4 PAYMENT PLAN API

POST /api/v1/payment-plans
Body:
{
  "patient_id": "uuid",
  "plan_type": "6_months",
  "total_amount": 600.00,
  "auto_pay_enabled": true
}

GET /api/v1/patients/{patient_id}/payment-plans
PUT /api/v1/payment-plans/{plan_id}/suspend
PUT /api/v1/payment-plans/{plan_id}/cancel

4.5 ADJUSTMENT & CLAIMS API

POST /api/v1/adjustments
Body:
{
  "patient_id": "uuid",
  "visit_id": "uuid",
  "adjustment_type": "professional_courtesy",
  "amount": 50.00,
  "reason": "Hardship case"
}

GET /api/v1/claims/{claim_id}
GET /api/v1/patients/{patient_id}/claims

5. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS & INTEGRATIONS
================================================================================

5.1 INSURANCE INTEGRATION
- Real-time eligibility API: Optum, Availity, Change Healthcare
- Fallback: SFTP batch files if real-time unavailable
- Handles multiple insurance networks (>500 payers)
- Cache eligibility for 30 days, refresh on demand
- Timeout handling: Return cached data if API exceeds 3 seconds

5.2 PAYMENT PROCESSING
- PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance: Tokenized processing via Stripe
- Payment processor webhook: Update payment status in real-time
- Retry logic: Failed payments retry 1x at 3 days, 1x at 7 days
- Fraud detection: Built-in Stripe fraud tools + custom velocity checks
- Supports US credit cards, international cards, ACH

5.3 BANKING & RECONCILIATION
- Bank API integration: Plaid for real-time bank data
- Automatic bank reconciliation: Nightly batch matching deposits
- Unmatched transaction alerts: Manual investigation needed
- Check imaging: Upload scanned checks for audit trail

5.4 COMPLIANCE & AUDIT
- HIPAA: All financial data encrypted at rest and in transit
- PCI-DSS: Tokenized card processing, no card storage
- SoC 2 Type II: Compliant audit logging on all financial transactions
- Regulatory reporting: Support for state insurance board audits
- Data retention: 7-year financial record retention (CMS requirement)

11. RISKS & MITIGATION
================================================================================

Risk: Insurance eligibility API downtime
Mitigation: Fallback to cached data (30-day cache), manual verification option

Risk: Payment processing failures (failed authorizations)
Mitigation: Automatic retry logic (2 retries over 10 days), SMS/email notifications

Risk: Insurance claim denials and appeals
Mitigation: Automated denial tracking, appeal workflow, denial reason analysis

Risk: PCI-DSS compliance complexity
Mitigation: Use tokenized payment processing (Stripe), avoid direct card handling

Risk: Reconciliation discrepancies with insurance
Mitigation: Manual reconciliation workflow, daily exception reporting
