================================================================================
1. OBJECTIVE STATEMENT
================================================================================

Implement comprehensive consultation module enabling doctors to document 
patient encounters in real-time. System must capture chief complaint, vital 
signs, examination findings, assessments, and create structured clinical 
documentation. Support multiple consultation types (new patient initial visit, 
follow-up, urgent care, telemedicine). Provide clinical decision support and 
ensure HIPAA-compliant audit trails for all documentation.

Expected Outcome:
- Complete consultation workflow from appointment → documentation → finalization
- Structured encounter notes supporting clinical decision-making
- Real-time vital sign capture and trending
- HIPAA-compliant encounter recording
- Integrated assessment and plan generation

================================================================================
2. BUSINESS LOGIC EXPLANATION
================================================================================

CONSULTATION LIFECYCLE:
1. Appointment Status Check
   - Verify appointment is CONFIRMED or IN_PROGRESS
   - Lock appointment from cancellation once consultation starts
   - Record consultation start timestamp
   - Retrieve patient's complete medical history context

2. Chief Complaint Capture
   - Doctor records patient's primary complaint
   - Free-text or structured symptom selection
   - Duration of symptom (days, weeks, months)
   - Severity rating (1-10 scale)
   - Associated symptoms listing
   - Impact on daily activities

3. Vital Signs Entry
   - Real-time capture: BP, HR, Temperature, O2 Sat, RR, Height, Weight
   - Automatic BMI calculation from height/weight
   - Flag abnormal values for immediate attention
   - Compare against patient baselines and normal ranges
   - Time-stamp each vital sign entry
   - Support manual entry and device integration

4. Physical Examination
   - Document systems reviewed (constitutional, eyes, ears, nose, throat, etc.)
   - Structured vs. free-text examination findings
   - Abnormal findings highlighted for clinician review
   - Cross-reference to potential diagnoses
   - Support for examination templates by body system

5. Assessment Generation
   - Doctor's clinical assessment of findings
   - Rule-out diagnoses list
   - Differential diagnosis support via clinical decision support (CDS)
   - Problem identification and severity assessment
   - Evidence-based recommendations for next steps

6. Plan Development
   - Treatment plan creation
   - Medication recommendations (pre-populated based on diagnosis)
   - Diagnostic test ordering (lab, imaging)
   - Referral recommendations to specialists
   - Patient education points
   - Follow-up scheduling (timeframe, type)

7. Consultation Finalization
   - Doctor attestation and signature (e-signature with timestamp)
   - Availability for patient review (with delay if needed)
   - Auto-creation of work items (prescriptions, orders, referrals)
   - Trigger follow-up appointment scheduling if needed
   - Update problem list and active medication list
   - Notify care team of any alerts or critical findings

CONSULTATION TYPES:

New Patient Initial Visit (90+ minutes)
- Comprehensive history taking (past medical history, surgical history, medications, allergies, family history, social history)
- Complete physical examination
- Initial assessment and problem list creation
- Comprehensive care plan development
- Baseline vital signs documentation
- Insurance and authorization verification

Follow-up Visit (20-30 minutes)
- Review of prior encounter findings
- Assessment of treatment plan compliance
- Repeat of relevant vital signs
- Brief focused examination based on chief complaint
- Updated assessment based on interim history
- Modified plan as needed

Urgent Care Visit (15-20 minutes)
- Rapid assessment of acute complaint
- Focused physical examination
- Immediate intervention or referral
- Brief documentation (streamlined for speed)
- Clear follow-up instructions

Telemedicine Consultation (varies)
- Limited physical examination (visual assessment only)
- Rely on vital signs provided by patient (if available)
- Video documentation of appearance and affect
- Written instructions for follow-up in-person exam if needed
- Recording for quality assurance and compliance

Post-Operative Follow-up (15-30 minutes)
- Assessment of surgical recovery
- Wound or incision site examination
- Pain level assessment
- Medication tolerance review
- Return-to-normal-activities guidance

5. WORKFLOW DESCRIPTION - STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS FLOW
================================================================================

NEW PATIENT INITIAL VISIT (90+ minutes):

Step 1: Appointment Start
  └─ Doctor clicks "Start Consultation" from appointment detail
  └─ System creates consultation record, sets status to IN_PROGRESS
  └─ Appointment locked from cancellation
  └─ Retrieve patient's complete medical history:
     ├─ Prior diagnoses and problems
     ├─ Current medications
     ├─ Allergies and adverse reactions
     ├─ Prior surgeries
     ├─ Immunization history
     └─ Family history

Step 2: Chief Complaint & History Taking
  └─ Doctor enters chief complaint ("Patient complains of...")
  └─ Enter symptom duration and severity
  └─ For new patient: Conduct comprehensive history taking
     ├─ Past medical history (PMH) - major illnesses, hospitalizations
     ├─ Past surgical history (PSH) - prior surgeries
     ├─ Medications - all current medications reviewed and reconciled
     ├─ Allergies - drug allergies and environmental allergies
     ├─ Family history - major diseases in relatives
     ├─ Social history - occupation, smoking, alcohol, drug use
     └─ Preventive health - last physical, immunizations, screenings

Step 3: Vital Signs Capture
  └─ Nurse or doctor enters vital signs:
     ├─ Blood pressure (Systolic/Diastolic)
     ├─ Heart rate (bpm)
     ├─ Temperature (°C)
     ├─ Respiratory rate (breaths/min)
     ├─ Oxygen saturation (%)
     └─ Height, weight (auto-calculates BMI)
  └─ System flags abnormal values:
     ├─ Red flag critical values (HR > 120, O2 Sat < 90%, Temp > 39)
     ├─ Yellow flag elevated values (BP elevated, BMI > 30)
     └─ Trending alerts (BP higher than baseline)

Step 4: Physical Examination
  └─ Doctor performs systematic physical examination:
     ├─ Constitutional (general appearance, distress level, affect)
     ├─ Eyes, Ears, Nose, Throat (HEENT)
     ├─ Cardiovascular (heart sounds, murmurs, pulses)
     ├─ Respiratory (lung sounds, wheezing, rales)
     ├─ Abdominal (palpation, tenderness, masses)
     ├─ Neurological (mentation, coordination, reflexes)
     ├─ Extremities (edema, pulses, wounds)
     └─ Skin (rashes, lesions, color)
  └─ For each system:
     ├─ Enter findings (normal vs abnormal)
     ├─ Flag abnormal findings for clinical action
     └─ System prompts for follow-up examination if indicated

Step 5: Assessment Development
  └─ Doctor enters clinical assessment:
     ├─ Summary of key findings
     ├─ Integration of history + examination + vital signs
     └─ Clinical reasoning process
  └─ Suggest differential diagnoses:
     ├─ Retrieve ICD-10 codes matching findings
     ├─ Rank primary vs differential vs ruled-out diagnoses
     └─ Request Clinical Decision Support (CDS) if needed
  └─ CDS engine analyzes:
     ├─ Patient findings vs knowledge base
     ├─ Recommends most probable diagnoses
     ├─ Suggests diagnostic tests
     └─ Alerts for drug interactions, contraindications
  └─ Doctor confirms diagnosis:
     ├─ Primary diagnosis (most likely)
     ├─ Differential diagnoses (possible alternatives)
     ├─ Ruled-out diagnoses (ruled out by examination)
     └─ Mark as NEW or existing problem

Step 6: Plan Development
  └─ Doctor creates treatment plan:
     ├─ Medications (recommendations pre-populated based on diagnosis)
     ├─ Diagnostic tests (lab, imaging, specialty tests)
     ├─ Referrals (to specialists if indicated)
     ├─ Procedures (if indicated)
     ├─ Patient education points
     └─ Follow-up instructions
  └─ For new patient:
     ├─ Establish baseline care plan
     ├─ Schedule routine preventive care (physical, screenings)
     └─ Establish follow-up schedule

Step 7: Documentation Review & Attestation
  └─ Doctor reviews complete consultation:
     ├─ Chief complaint and history
     ├─ Vital signs and findings
     ├─ Assessment and plan
     └─ Actions and recommendations
  └─ Doctor makes corrections if needed:
     ├─ Draft auto-saved every 30 seconds
     ├─ Can restore from any auto-saved version
     └─ Final review before finalization
  └─ Doctor signs consultation:
     ├─ Electronic signature with timestamp
     ├─ Sets as FINALIZED (locked from editing)
     └─ Generates permanent clinical record

Step 8: Finalization & Work Item Creation
  └─ System creates work items from plan:
     ├─ Prescription orders → sent to pharmacy
     ├─ Lab orders → sent to lab for processing
     ├─ Imaging orders → sent to radiology
     ├─ Referrals → sent to receiving provider
     └─ Follow-up appointments → scheduled automatically
  └─ Patient notifications:
     ├─ Email notification of new prescriptions ready for pickup
     ├─ Lab results notification when available
     ├─ Follow-up appointment reminders
     └─ Consultation note access (with optional delay)
  └─ Care team notifications:
     ├─ Alerts for abnormal findings requiring action
     ├─ Updates to problem list and medication list
     └─ Alerts for critical values or flags

FOLLOW-UP VISIT (20-30 minutes):

Step 1-2: [Same as above - chief complaint]

Step 3-4: [Same as above - vital signs, focused examination]

Step 5-6: [Brief assessment and plan update]

Step 7-8: [Documentation, attestation, finalization]

Key Differences:
  ├─ Limited history taking (focus on interval history since last visit)
  ├─ Focused examination (on chief complaint area)
  ├─ Review of treatment compliance with prior plan
  ├─ Assessment of response to prior treatment
  └─ Plan modifications based on response

URGENT CARE VISIT (15-20 minutes):

Key Differences:
  ├─ Rapid assessment (minimal history taking)
  ├─ Focused examination (on acute complaint only)
  ├─ Rapid decision-making (immediate intervention or referral)
  ├─ Streamlined documentation (quick note, brief plan)
  ├─ High alert threshold (critical findings escalated immediately)
  └─ Clear discharge instructions (follow-up, return precautions)

6. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS & CONSTRAINTS
================================================================================

PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS:
- Consultation list load: < 2 seconds for 100+ consultations
- Clinical summary retrieval: < 1 second (vital signs, findings, diagnoses)
- Vital sign entry: < 500ms from data entry to confirmation
- Finding documentation: < 700ms from entry to display
- CDS analysis: < 3 seconds for diagnosis recommendations
- Consultation finalization: < 2 seconds including work item creation
- Draft auto-save: < 200ms every 30 seconds without blocking UI

SECURITY & COMPLIANCE:
- All consultation data encrypted at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- HIPAA compliance:
  * Audit log of all access to consultation (who, when, what)
  * Access control by role (doctor, nurse, patient, admin)
  * Automatic logging of failed access attempts
  * Consultation notes withheld until provider configurable delay (0-72 hours)
  * Restrict patient access to own consultations only
- Patient privacy:
  * Consultation not visible to patient by default
  * Doctor must explicitly mark "visible_to_patient" before patient can access
  * Optional delay (24-72 hours) before patient sees consultation
  * Option to redact sensitive sections before patient visibility
- Concurrent edit prevention:
  * Only one doctor can actively edit consultation at a time
  * Lock consultation during active editing (prevents race conditions)
  * Warn if another doctor is editing same consultation
  * Auto-save prevents data loss on connection interruption

ACCESSIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
- Consultation form must be WCAG 2.1 AA compliant
- Keyboard navigation for all form fields
- Screen reader compatible for vital sign entry and findings
- High contrast mode support
- Text size adjustability (100%-200%)
- Speech recognition for voice input (optional telemedicine feature)

INTEGRATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Integrate with Task 22 (Prescription System) - create prescriptions from plan
- Integrate with Task 23 (Diagnosis Recording) - feed diagnosis into problem list
- Integrate with Task 24 (Clinical Notes) - create note templates from consultation

12. RISKS & MITIGATION
================================================================================

RISK 1: CDS Engine Performance
  │ Impact: If CDS analysis takes > 3 seconds, blocks doctor workflow
  │ Probability: MEDIUM
  │ Mitigation:
  │  ├─ Pre-calculate common diagnosis patterns
  │  ├─ Cache diagnosis recommendations
  │  ├─ Implement timeout (3 seconds max)
  │  └─ Fall back to manual entry if CDS timeout

RISK 2: Vital Sign Abnormality Alert Threshold
  │ Impact: Too sensitive → alert fatigue; Too lenient → miss abnormalities
  │ Probability: MEDIUM
  │ Mitigation:
  │  ├─ Configurable alert thresholds per clinic
  │  ├─ Machine learning to personalize thresholds
  │  ├─ Beta testing with pilot clinics
  │  └─ Continuous monitoring and refinement

RISK 3: Draft Auto-Save on Slow Network
  │ Impact: Auto-save every 30s may conflict with active editing
  │ Probability: LOW
  │ Mitigation:
  │  ├─ Async auto-save (non-blocking)
  │  ├─ Debounce auto-save (wait for typing pause)
  │  ├─ Queue unsaved changes if network unavailable
  │  └─ Notify user of sync status

RISK 4: Concurrent Edit Conflicts
  │ Impact: Two doctors editing same consultation simultaneously
  │ Probability: LOW (but possible with shift handovers)
  │ Mitigation:
  │  ├─ Lock consultation during active editing
  │  ├─ Warn user if another doctor is editing
  │  ├─ Implement conflict resolution (last-write-wins or manual merge)
  │  └─ Audit trail of all changes

RISK 5: Data Entry Errors (Wrong Diagnosis Code, Drug Allergy Override)
  │ Impact: Patient safety risk, medication errors, wrong treatment
  │ Probability: MEDIUM (human factors)
  │ Mitigation:
  │  ├─ Confirmation dialogs for critical changes
  │  ├─ Drug allergy checking with override warnings
  │  ├─ ICD-10 code verification with lookup tool
  │  ├─ Peer review before finalization (optional)
  │  └─ Audit trail for accountability

14. ADDITIONAL NOTES & CONTEXT
================================================================================

CONSULTATION TYPE VARIATIONS:

New Patient Visit (90+ minutes):
  - Comprehensive history and physical
  - Establishes baseline health status
  - Creates problem list
  - Establishes care plan
  - Highest documentation time

Follow-up Visit (20-30 minutes):
  - Focused on chief complaint
  - Brief interval history
  - Assess treatment response
  - Modify plan as needed
  - Most common consultation type

Urgent Care Visit (15-20 minutes):
  - Rapid assessment
  - Immediate intervention or referral
  - Streamlined documentation
  - May be incomplete (transferred to hospital)

Telemedicine Consultation:
  - Limited to visual assessment
  - Vital signs from patient report
  - Clear instructions for in-person follow-up if needed
  - Security/privacy considerations

Post-Operative Follow-up:
  - Specific focus on surgical recovery
  - Wound/incision assessment
  - Pain management review
  - Return-to-activity guidance

CLINICAL DECISION SUPPORT (CDS):

The Consultation Module integrates with a Clinical Decision Support engine that:
  ├─ Analyzes patient findings against medical knowledge base
  ├─ Recommends diagnoses ranked by probability
  ├─ Suggests diagnostic tests (lab, imaging)
  ├─ Checks medication interactions
  ├─ Checks drug-allergy contraindications
  ├─ Checks drug-disease contraindications
  ├─ Provides evidence-based treatment recommendations
  └─ Flags critical findings requiring escalation

CDS recommendations are ADVISORY ONLY. Doctor retains full authority over diagnosis
and treatment decisions. All CDS rejections/overrides logged for quality review.

TEMPLATE SYSTEM:

Consultation module supports templates for common visit types:
  ├─ New patient intake template (comprehensive history/exam)
  ├─ Follow-up visit template (abbreviated)
  ├─ Urgent care template (rapid)
  ├─ Telemedicine template (visual assessment only)
  ├─ Post-op follow-up template (recovery assessment)
  └─ Custom templates per clinic

Templates pre-populate form sections to reduce data entry time and improve consistency.

AUDIT & ACCOUNTABILITY:

Every consultation action logged:
  ├─ Who: User ID and role
  ├─ What: Action (create, modify, finalize, view)
  ├─ When: Timestamp (audit trail)
  ├─ Where: IP address, device, browser
  └─ Why: Reason code (routine, administrative, legal)

Critical actions logged for compliance:
  ├─ Consultation finalization
  ├─ Drug allergy overrides
  ├─ CDS recommendation rejections
  ├─ Consultation visibility changes
  ├─ Patient access to consultation
  └─ Any access by non-treating physician
