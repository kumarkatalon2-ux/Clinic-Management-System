1. OBJECTIVE STATEMENT
================================================================================

The Follow-up Automation System manages patient follow-up workflows including
appointment scheduling, patient outreach, clinical reminders, and care coordination.
It automatically identifies patients requiring follow-up based on clinical protocols,
schedules appointments or sends notifications, tracks follow-up completion, and
escalates cases when follow-up is missed. The system improves patient engagement,
ensures continuity of care, reduces complications from missed follow-ups, and
provides data on follow-up compliance for quality reporting.

Key Responsibilities:
- Automatic follow-up scheduling based on diagnosis and treatment plan
- Clinical protocol-driven follow-up timing (e.g., post-op check in 2 weeks)
- Multi-channel patient outreach (portal notification, SMS, email, phone call)
- Follow-up reminder notifications (appointment reminder, medication refill)
- Care coordination between providers (specialist referrals, care team)
- Escalation alerts (patient missed follow-up appointment)
- Follow-up compliance tracking and reporting
- Patient engagement analytics

2. BUSINESS LOGIC & WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

2.1 FOLLOW-UP PROTOCOL DEFINITION
Clinical Protocols:
- Post-operative: Follow-up 2 weeks after surgery (wound check, suture removal)
- Post-prescription: Follow-up 4 weeks after starting new medication (efficacy, side effects)
- Chronic disease: Follow-up per condition (diabetes Q3 months, hypertension Q6 months)
- Lab results: Follow-up when abnormal results returned (discuss findings, action plan)
- Referral: Follow-up 2 weeks after specialist referral (obtain specialist report)
- Discharge: Follow-up 1 week after hospital discharge (transition of care)
- Inpatient: Follow-up 1 week after clinic discharge (urgent care episode)

Protocol Customization:
- Configurable by diagnosis, treatment type, provider preference
- Evidence-based defaults from clinical guidelines
- Manager/clinical director override for specific protocols
- A/B testing: Different follow-up timing to optimize outcomes

2.2 AUTOMATIC FOLLOW-UP SCHEDULING
Trigger Events:
- Consultation completion → Check treatment plan for follow-up orders
- Prescription issued → Check medication type for post-med follow-up
- Procedure performed → Schedule post-procedure follow-up
- Lab results abnormal → Flag for follow-up discussion
- Specialist referral sent → Track for follow-up report
- Hospital discharge → Schedule transition of care visit

Follow-up Order Entry (by clinician):
- Type: Appointment (face-to-face), virtual (telemedicine), phone (check-in)
- Timing: 2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months (or custom)
- Provider: Same provider or any provider with specialty
- Visit type: Routine follow-up, urgent follow-up, medication review
- Instructions: "Check wound healing", "Assess medication efficacy", "Review lab results"

Automatic Scheduling:
- System receives follow-up order at consultation/procedure end
- Calculates target date (e.g., 2 weeks from today)
- Checks provider availability on target date (±3 days flexibility)
- Books earliest available appointment within window
- Sends patient appointment confirmation (portal, email, SMS)
- If no availability: Creates waitlist entry, alerts patient to call for scheduling

2.3 PATIENT OUTREACH & ENGAGEMENT
Appointment Reminders:
- Automated reminders at 7 days before appointment
- Reminder channels: SMS (preferred), email (fallback), portal notification
- Content: "Reminder: You have follow-up appointment [date] [time] with Dr. Smith"
- One-click reschedule: Patient can click to reschedule if unable to attend
- Two-way: Patient can confirm attendance or request change

Pre-Appointment Outreach:
- 1 week before: Appointment reminder
- 3 days before: "Do you still need this appointment?" (reduce no-shows)
- If patient confirms unable to attend: Offer to reschedule
- If patient unresponsive at 3 days: Phone call from clinic (if high-priority)

Post-Visit Follow-up:
- Immediate: Patient education materials sent (post-op care, medication info)
- 24 hours: "How was your visit?" satisfaction survey
- 3 days: Clinical questions (any problems, side effects, questions)
- Weekly: Check-in for high-risk patients (post-op, new medication)
- Monthly: Outcome assessment (symptom improvement, goal achievement)

2.4 CLINICAL REMINDERS & ALERTS
At-Risk Patient Identification:
- Rules engine: Identify patients meeting risk criteria
- Examples: Diabetics due for HbA1c, hypertensive overdue for BP check
- Priority: Urgent (overdue >1 month), high (due within 1 week), normal (due within 1 month)
- Provider alert: Dashboard notification of due/overdue patients

Medication Refill Reminders:
- Based on medication dispensing date + days supply
- Reminder sent 7 days before expected supply runs out
- Channels: SMS, email, patient portal
- Patient can request refill via portal, SMS, or clinic call
- Automatic refill enabled for chronic medications (patient opt-in)

Lab Results Follow-up:
- Abnormal result identified → Doctor notified
- Doctor enters follow-up order (discuss result, repeat test, lifestyle change)
- Follow-up reminder to patient: "Your [lab] result came back. Schedule appointment to discuss."
- Doctor appointment queue: "Results to discuss: patient X"
- Patient outreach: SMS/email/call if patient doesn't schedule within 3 days

2.5 CARE COORDINATION
Internal Care Team:
- When referred to specialist: Track referral status
- Get specialist report: Flag for chart review, incorporation into care plan
- Care team assignments: Nurse coordinator, case manager, social worker
- Collaborative notes: Multi-provider communication about patient care

Specialist Referrals:
- Provider submits referral with clinical justification
- Insurance pre-auth obtained (if required)
- Patient notified of referral and specialist contact info
- Specialist appointment booked (if in-network)
- Tracking: Follow-up with patient on appointment date
- Report retrieval: Automatic follow-up to obtain specialist report

Care Coordination Workflows:
- Complex patient: Assign care coordinator
- Coordinator schedules multi-provider conference call
- Coord tracks care plan implementation
- Coord manages patient engagement (frequent check-ins, barrier removal)
- Outcome: Improved compliance, better outcomes

2.6 ESCALATION & NON-COMPLIANCE
Missed Appointment Follow-up:
- Patient misses appointment → System flags
- Automatic action: Reschedule attempt (portal/SMS/email)
- No response at 24 hours: Phone call from clinic
- No response at 48 hours: Escalate to nurse/manager for outreach
- Document reason (patient cancelled, patient unresponsive, etc.)
- Pattern detection: If repeated no-shows, review care plan

Non-Compliance Patterns:
- Track: Missed appointments, missed medications, lab non-adherence
- Flag: Patient with pattern of non-compliance (3+ missed appointments in 6 months)
- Intervention: Nurse/case manager outreach
- Root cause analysis: Barriers to engagement (transportation, cost, understanding)
- Solutions: Telemedicine if transportation barrier, financial assistance if cost barrier

Escalation Levels:
- Level 1: Auto-reminder (SMS/email)
- Level 2: Manual outreach (phone call from clinic staff)
- Level 3: Care coordinator engagement (home visit, intensive support)
- Level 4: Provider escalation (discuss care plan changes, higher intensity intervention)

2.7 FOLLOW-UP TRACKING & COMPLIANCE
Follow-up Completion Tracking:
- Track: Scheduled follow-up appointment vs actual completion
- Measure: Follow-up compliance rate (% of ordered follow-ups completed)
- Delay tracking: How long after recommended date was follow-up completed
- Breakdown: By diagnosis, provider, visit type, patient demographics

Non-Completion Investigation:
- Categorize: No-show, refused, patient unavailable, provider cancelled
- Outcome: If patient never completes, document and escalate
- Pattern: If diagnosis or provider has low compliance, investigate process

Quality Metrics:
- Follow-up compliance rate: >90% of ordered follow-ups completed
- Time to follow-up: Average delay from recommended date
- Patient satisfaction: Ease of scheduling, communication quality
- Clinical outcomes: Link follow-up to patient outcomes (readmission, complications)

2.8 ANALYTICS & REPORTING
Follow-up Metrics:
- Total follow-ups ordered vs completed
- Average follow-up compliance by diagnosis
- Average follow-up compliance by provider
- Follow-up wait time (from recommendation to completion)
- No-show rate for follow-up appointments
- Patient engagement (response rate to reminders)

Predictive Analytics:
- No-follow-up risk: Predict patients unlikely to follow-up
- Non-compliance risk: Predict patients at risk for missing appointments
- Intervention: Target outreach to high-risk patients

Reporting:
- Provider dashboard: Follow-ups due, pending, completed
- Manager report: Compliance by provider, by department
- Quality report: Follow-up compliance vs clinical outcomes
- Patient report: Personal follow-up summary, upcoming appointments

3. DATA MODEL & SCHEMA
================================================================================

3.1 CORE TABLES

TABLE: follow_up_protocols
- protocol_id (UUID, PK)
- diagnosis_code (VARCHAR, ICD-10)
- condition_name (VARCHAR)
- follow_up_type (ENUM: "appointment", "phone_call", "virtual_visit")
- timing_days (INT, e.g., 14 for 2 weeks)
- timing_range (INT, flexibility ±days)
- priority_level (ENUM: "urgent", "high", "normal")
- clinical_rationale (TEXT)
- is_active (BOOLEAN)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)

TABLE: follow_up_orders
- order_id (UUID, PK)
- visit_id (UUID, FK → consultations)
- patient_id (UUID, FK → patients)
- provider_id (UUID, FK → providers)
- follow_up_type (ENUM: "appointment", "phone", "video", "in_house")
- ordered_date (DATE)
- target_date (DATE, recommended follow-up date)
- provider_preference (VARCHAR, nullable)
- visit_type (VARCHAR, e.g., "post-med check", "wound check")
- clinical_instructions (TEXT)
- status (ENUM: "pending", "scheduled", "completed", "cancelled", "overdue")
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- INDEX: (patient_id, status, target_date)

TABLE: follow_up_appointments
- appointment_id (UUID, PK)
- order_id (UUID, FK → follow_up_orders)
- appointment_date (DATE)
- appointment_time (TIME)
- provider_id (UUID, FK → providers)
- status (ENUM: "scheduled", "completed", "no_show", "cancelled")
- patient_confirmed (BOOLEAN)
- confirmation_date (TIMESTAMP, nullable)
- completion_date (TIMESTAMP, nullable)
- notes (TEXT)
- INDEX: (order_id, appointment_date)

TABLE: patient_outreach
- outreach_id (UUID, PK)
- patient_id (UUID, FK → patients)
- outreach_type (ENUM: "appointment_reminder", "medication_refill", "lab_result", "follow_up_check_in")
- channel (ENUM: "sms", "email", "portal", "phone_call")
- message (TEXT)
- scheduled_date (DATE)
- sent_date (TIMESTAMP, nullable)
- response_received (BOOLEAN)
- response_content (TEXT, nullable)
- response_date (TIMESTAMP, nullable)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- INDEX: (patient_id, outreach_type, sent_date)

TABLE: care_coordination
- coordination_id (UUID, PK)
- patient_id (UUID, FK → patients)
- coordinator_id (UUID, FK → users)
- start_date (DATE)
- end_date (DATE, nullable)
- reason (VARCHAR, e.g., "Complex patient", "Post-op")
- status (ENUM: "active", "completed", "on_hold")
- care_plan (TEXT)
- index: (patient_id, status)

TABLE: follow_up_compliance
- compliance_id (UUID, PK)
- patient_id (UUID, FK → patients)
- tracking_period_start (DATE)
- tracking_period_end (DATE)
- follow_ups_ordered (INT)
- follow_ups_completed (INT)
- compliance_rate (DECIMAL 5,2, e.g., 92.5)
- on_time_count (INT, completed within 7 days of target)
- overdue_count (INT, completed >7 days late)

4. API SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

4.1 FOLLOW-UP ORDER API

POST /api/v1/follow-up/orders
Request:
{
  "visit_id": "uuid",
  "follow_up_type": "appointment",
  "target_date": "2026-02-14",
  "clinical_instructions": "Post-op wound check"
}
Response: Follow-up order created

GET /api/v1/follow-up/orders/{patient_id}
Response: All follow-up orders for patient

4.2 APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING API

POST /api/v1/follow-up/schedule-appointment
Body:
{
  "order_id": "uuid",
  "provider_id": "uuid",
  "appointment_date": "2026-02-14",
  "time": "14:00"
}
Response: Appointment scheduled

PUT /api/v1/follow-up/appointments/{appointment_id}/mark-completed

4.3 PATIENT OUTREACH API

POST /api/v1/follow-up/outreach
Body: { patient_id, outreach_type, channel, message }

GET /api/v1/follow-up/outreach/response-rate
Query: date_range, outreach_type

4.4 COMPLIANCE TRACKING API

GET /api/v1/follow-up/compliance/{patient_id}
Response: Follow-up compliance metrics

GET /api/v1/follow-up/compliance/provider/{provider_id}
Response: Provider's follow-up compliance

5.1 SCHEDULING ENGINE
- Integration with appointment scheduling system (Task 17)
- Automatic slot finder: Find earliest available within date range
- Conflict detection: Check provider availability, room availability
- Multi-provider support: Route to same provider or available provider
- Fallback: Waitlist if no availability

- Scheduling: Send at optimal times (avoid evening/weekends)

5.3 CLINICAL PROTOCOL ENGINE
- Configurable rules: Define follow-up protocols per diagnosis
- Evidence-based defaults: HEDIS measures, clinical guidelines
- Override capability: Allow exceptions per patient/provider
- Versioning: Track protocol changes, audit trail
