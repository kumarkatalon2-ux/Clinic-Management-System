1. OBJECTIVE STATEMENT
================================================================================

The Inventory Management System tracks medical supplies, pharmaceuticals, diagnostic
materials, and clinic equipment inventory. It manages stock levels, ordering,
receiving, consumption tracking, expiration date management, lot tracking for
compliance, and automated reorder points. The system provides real-time visibility
of inventory status, generates reorder alerts, maintains FIFO (first-in-first-out)
dispensing, and ensures regulatory compliance for medical devices and controlled
substances.

Key Responsibilities:
- Real-time inventory tracking by location (main clinic, exam rooms, pharmacy)
- Automated reorder point triggers with purchase requisition generation
- Supplier management and purchase order processing
- Receiving workflow with barcode scanning
- Stock movement tracking (dispensing, wastage, returns, adjustments)
- Expiration date management and recall processing
- Lot/serial tracking for regulatory compliance and traceability
- Barcode generation and scanning for inventory operations
- Multi-location transfer and distribution
- Consumption analytics and cost tracking

2. BUSINESS LOGIC & WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

2.1 INVENTORY CATEGORIZATION
Four Main Categories:
- Medical Supplies: Bandages, gauze, syringes, catheters, gloves, PPE
- Pharmaceuticals: Non-controlled medications (antibiotics, antihistamines)
- Controlled Substances: Scheduled medications (opioids, benzodiazepines, stimulants)
- Equipment: Diagnostic devices, monitors, examination equipment
- Lab Materials: Culture media, reagents, reference materials

Each Category Specifics:
- Medical Supplies: FIFO rotation, expiration tracking, quantity alerts
- Pharmaceuticals: Expiration critical, lot tracking, interaction checks
- Controlled Substances: DEA tracking, limited storage, usage logging, audit trails
- Equipment: Maintenance scheduling, calibration tracking, depreciation
- Lab Materials: Stability requirements, storage conditions, shelf life

2.2 INITIAL STOCK SETUP
Stock Creation Process:
- Item master data: Name, SKU, category, unit, description
- Reorder point: Minimum quantity before reorder (e.g., 20 units)
- Reorder quantity: Standard order amount (e.g., 100 units)
- Safety stock: Extra buffer for emergencies (5-10% above reorder point)
- Expiration tracking: Flag if item expires (date-tracked or shelf-life tracked)
- Storage location: Primary storage location, backup locations
- Unit cost: Cost per unit from supplier
- Preferred supplier: Default supplier for ordering

Initial Stock Entry:
- Manual entry for existing inventory at system launch
- Barcode scanning for verification and accuracy
- Photo documentation for expensive equipment
- Physical count verification by two staff members

2.3 AUTOMATED REORDER WORKFLOWS
Reorder Trigger:
- Daily check: Compare current stock vs reorder point
- Alert generated: "Item X stock below reorder point (5 units, reorder: 20)"
- Automatic PO generation: Creates purchase order if enabled
- Manual approval: Manager approves PO before sending to supplier
- Procurement sends to supplier via EDI, email, or phone

Reorder Point Calculation:
- Lead time (days supplier takes to deliver): 5 days
- Daily consumption: 4 units
- Reorder point = lead time × daily consumption + safety stock
- Example: 5 × 4 + 10 = 30 units minimum
- Adjusts seasonally (higher before flu season, lower in summer)

PO Management:
- Purchase order created with supplier details
- Expected delivery date calculated from lead time
- Manager review and approval workflow
- Supplier acknowledgment tracking
- Follow-up alerts if delivery overdue

2.4 RECEIVING & INTAKE WORKFLOW
Receiving Process:
- Supplier shipment arrives with packing slip
- Receiving staff scans shipment barcode (if multiple items)
- Scans each item's barcode to verify quantity and match PO
- System alerts on quantity discrepancies
- Verifies expiration dates (flags if <3 months from expiry)
- Checks for damage or quality issues
- Logs received items into inventory system

Intake Actions:
- Barcode label generation for items without barcodes (in-house generated)
- Bin location assignment (rack A1, shelf 2, etc.)
- Expiration date entry for tracked items
- Lot number entry for controlled substances and pharmaceuticals
- Serial number entry for equipment
- Photo documentation for equipment
- System updates inventory balance and reorder point
- Receiving complete and inventory available for use

2.5 CONSUMPTION & DISPENSING
Daily Consumption Tracking:
- Dispensing from clinic: Staff scans item barcode and quantity dispensed
- System updates real-time inventory balance
- Links dispensing to patient visit (for usage tracking and auditing)
- Supports partial item usage (e.g., half a box of gloves)
- FIFO enforcement: System prioritizes oldest stock first

Consumption Scenarios:
- Exam room supply: Gloves, syringes, bandages (via barcode)
- Pharmacy dispense: Medications via pharmacy system (integrated)
- Lab usage: Culture media, reagents via lab ordering
- Procedure waste: Documented waste per procedure type
- Emergency use: Override dispensing for urgent needs (audit logged)

Consumption Analytics:
- Daily usage patterns by category
- Cost per encounter (supplies used per visit)
- Departmental consumption (main clinic vs urgent care)
- Waste tracking and minimization

2.6 EXPIRATION & RECALL MANAGEMENT
Expiration Date Tracking:
- All items with expiration dates tracked
- Real-time expiration alerts:
  * 60 days before: Yellow flag ("Use by date approaching")
  * 30 days before: Red flag ("Item expiring soon")
  * On expiration date: Item locked from use
- Automatic quarantine of expired items
- Disposal tracking and documentation

Recall Processing:
- Supplier recall notification received
- System searches for affected lot numbers or serial numbers
- Quarantine workflow: Items flagged as recalled, removed from circulation
- Notification to relevant staff (pharmacy, clinic managers)
- Documentation of returned items to supplier
- Refund processing with supplier
- Replacement order submitted

2.7 MULTI-LOCATION INVENTORY
Location Management:
- Main clinic: Central pharmacy and supply room
- Exam rooms (4): Individual exam supply cabinets
- Urgent care: Separate urgent care supply room
- Storage warehouse: Off-site backup storage
- Each location: Separate inventory tracking, reorder points, alerts

Transfer Workflow:
- Transfer initiated from source location to destination
- Barcode scanning of items being transferred
- Quantity verification
- In-transit status
- Receipt confirmation at destination
- System adjusts inventory in both locations

Centralized Visibility:
- Dashboard shows inventory at all locations
- Global inventory balance across all locations
- Reorder alerts trigger based on total inventory
- Transfer recommendations (consolidate, redistribute)

2.8 CONTROLLED SUBSTANCE TRACKING
DEA Compliance:
- Separate tracking for DEA Schedule II-V controlled substances
- Each transaction logged with timestamp, user, quantity, reason
- Physical count verification (monthly physical vs system)
- Variance investigation and documentation
- Auditable chain of custody

Controlled Substance Records:
- Receipt: Item logged with lot number, quantity, date
- Dispensing: Usage logged with patient visit, dosage, route
- Destruction: Damaged/expired items logged with disposal method
- Transfers: Inter-location transfers documented
- Loss: Any theft or loss reported to DEA, documented

DEA Audit Support:
- Daily usage report by substance
- Monthly inventory count vs system reconciliation
- Annual usage summaries for DEA reporting
- Complete transaction audit trail

2.9 WASTAGE & ADJUSTMENTS
Wastage Documentation:
- Expired items: Reason (expiration), date, quantity, cost
- Damaged items: Reason (damage), description, quantity, cost
- Spillage/breakage: Reason, circumstances, quantity, cost
- Theft/loss: Reported with investigation details
- Adjustment: Correction of inventory discrepancy (reason required)

Wastage Analysis:
- Monthly wastage report by category
- Highest waste items (identify process improvement opportunities)
- Cost of wastage (identify expensive waste items)
- Wastage trends (increasing/decreasing)

Manager Approval:
- Wastage/adjustments >$500 require manager approval
- Theft/loss >$100 require incident documentation
- Approval workflow tracks who authorized adjustment

3. DATA MODEL & SCHEMA
================================================================================

3.1 CORE TABLES

TABLE: inventory_items
- item_id (UUID, PK)
- sku (VARCHAR, unique)
- item_name (VARCHAR)
- category (ENUM: "medical_supply", "pharmaceutical", "controlled_substance", "equipment", "lab_material")
- unit (ENUM: "box", "unit", "vial", "package", "case", "bottle", "each")
- description (TEXT)
- reorder_point (INT)
- reorder_quantity (INT)
- safety_stock (INT)
- unit_cost (DECIMAL 10,4)
- expires (BOOLEAN, true if item has expiration date)
- shelf_life_days (INT, nullable, if not expires_at date)
- preferred_supplier_id (UUID, FK → suppliers)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- INDEX: (sku, category)

TABLE: inventory_stock
- stock_id (UUID, PK)
- item_id (UUID, FK → inventory_items)
- location (ENUM: "main_clinic", "exam_room_1", "exam_room_2", "exam_room_3", "exam_room_4", "urgent_care", "warehouse")
- quantity_on_hand (INT)
- quantity_reserved (INT)
- available_quantity (INT, generated: on_hand - reserved)
- last_counted_at (TIMESTAMP)
- last_counted_by (UUID, FK → users)
- reorder_status (ENUM: "in_stock", "low_stock", "reorder_pending", "critical")
- updated_at (TIMESTAMP)
- INDEX: (item_id, location)

TABLE: inventory_lots
- lot_id (UUID, PK)
- item_id (UUID, FK → inventory_items)
- lot_number (VARCHAR)
- serial_number (VARCHAR, nullable)
- quantity (INT)
- received_date (DATE)
- expiration_date (DATE, nullable)
- manufactured_date (DATE, nullable)
- location (VARCHAR, e.g., "Rack A, Shelf 2")
- status (ENUM: "active", "low_stock", "expired", "quarantined", "recalled")
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- INDEX: (item_id, expiration_date, status)

TABLE: purchase_orders
- po_id (UUID, PK)
- supplier_id (UUID, FK → suppliers)
- po_number (VARCHAR, unique)
- order_date (DATE)
- expected_delivery_date (DATE)
- actual_delivery_date (DATE, nullable)
- status (ENUM: "draft", "pending_approval", "approved", "sent", "received", "cancelled")
- approved_by (UUID, FK → users, nullable)
- approved_at (TIMESTAMP, nullable)
- total_amount (DECIMAL 10,2)
- created_by (UUID, FK → users)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- INDEX: (supplier_id, order_date, status)

TABLE: po_line_items
- line_item_id (UUID, PK)
- po_id (UUID, FK → purchase_orders)
- item_id (UUID, FK → inventory_items)
- sku (VARCHAR)
- quantity_ordered (INT)
- quantity_received (INT)
- unit_cost (DECIMAL 10,4)
- line_total (DECIMAL 10,2)

TABLE: inventory_transactions
- transaction_id (UUID, PK)
- item_id (UUID, FK → inventory_items)
- lot_id (UUID, FK → inventory_lots, nullable)
- transaction_type (ENUM: "receive", "dispense", "transfer", "adjustment", "waste", "recall", "count")
- quantity (INT)
- reason (VARCHAR)
- reference_id (VARCHAR, nullable, e.g., visit_id, po_id, waste_id)
- from_location (VARCHAR, nullable)
- to_location (VARCHAR, nullable)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- created_by (UUID, FK → users)
- requires_approval (BOOLEAN, false unless wastage >$500)
- approved_by (UUID, FK → users, nullable)
- approved_at (TIMESTAMP, nullable)
- INDEX: (item_id, transaction_type, created_at)

TABLE: inventory_waste
- waste_id (UUID, PK)
- item_id (UUID, FK → inventory_items)
- lot_id (UUID, FK → inventory_lots, nullable)
- waste_type (ENUM: "expired", "damaged", "spillage", "theft", "loss", "adjustment")
- quantity (INT)
- cost (DECIMAL 10,2)
- reason (TEXT)
- incident_description (TEXT, nullable)
- status (ENUM: "pending_approval", "approved", "documented", "disposed")
- requested_by (UUID, FK → users)
- approved_by (UUID, FK → users, nullable)
- approved_at (TIMESTAMP, nullable)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- INDEX: (item_id, waste_type, created_at)

TABLE: suppliers
- supplier_id (UUID, PK)
- supplier_name (VARCHAR)
- contact_person (VARCHAR)
- phone (VARCHAR)
- email (VARCHAR)
- address (VARCHAR)
- lead_time_days (INT)
- payment_terms (VARCHAR, e.g., "Net 30")
- is_active (BOOLEAN)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- INDEX: (supplier_name)

4. API SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

4.1 INVENTORY LOOKUP API

GET /api/v1/inventory/items
Query params: category, search_term, low_stock_only
Response:
[
  {
    "item_id": "uuid",
    "sku": "GLV-001",
    "item_name": "Medical Gloves (Box of 100)",
    "quantity_on_hand": 5,
    "reorder_point": 20,
    "reorder_status": "critical"
  }
]

GET /api/v1/inventory/items/{item_id}/stock
Response: Stock by location

4.2 RECEIVING API

POST /api/v1/inventory/receiving
Body:
{
  "po_id": "uuid",
  "items": [
    {
      "item_id": "uuid",
      "quantity_received": 100,
      "expiration_date": "2026-06-30",
      "lot_number": "LOT-12345"
    }
  ]
}

4.3 DISPENSING API

POST /api/v1/inventory/dispense
Body:
{
  "visit_id": "uuid",
  "items": [
    {
      "item_id": "uuid",
      "quantity": 5,
      "reason": "Exam supply"
    }
  ]
}

4.4 TRANSFER API

POST /api/v1/inventory/transfer
Body:
{
  "item_id": "uuid",
  "quantity": 10,
  "from_location": "main_clinic",
  "to_location": "exam_room_1"
}

4.5 WASTAGE API

POST /api/v1/inventory/waste
Body:
{
  "item_id": "uuid",
  "waste_type": "expired",
  "quantity": 5,
  "reason": "Expiration date passed"
}

4.6 PURCHASE ORDER API

POST /api/v1/inventory/purchase-orders
Body:
{
  "supplier_id": "uuid",
  "items": [
    {
      "item_id": "uuid",
      "quantity": 100
    }
  ]
}

GET /api/v1/inventory/purchase-orders
Query: status, supplier_id, date_range

PUT /api/v1/inventory/purchase-orders/{po_id}/approve
PUT /api/v1/inventory/purchase-orders/{po_id}/cancel

4.7 ANALYTICS API

GET /api/v1/inventory/analytics/reorder-status
GET /api/v1/inventory/analytics/expiration-alerts
GET /api/v1/inventory/analytics/wastage-report
GET /api/v1/inventory/analytics/consumption-trends

5. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS & INTEGRATIONS
================================================================================

5.1 BARCODE SCANNING
- Barcode generation: EAN-13 or Code 128 format
- Barcode labels: Auto-print for received items
- Barcode readers: Handheld wireless scanners (Zebra TC25, Symbol MC32N)
- Scanning workflow: Scan item on receipt, scan when dispensing
- Mobile app: Barcode scanning via phone camera
- Quantity input: After scanning, staff enters quantity

5.2 ALERTS & NOTIFICATIONS
- Stock level alerts: Email to manager when below reorder point
- Expiration alerts: Daily report of items expiring within 30 days
- Low stock alerts: Real-time dashboard alerts
- PO overdue alerts: Follow-up alert if PO not received by expected date
- Recall alerts: Immediate notification when recall issued

5.3 REPORTING & ANALYTICS
- Daily consumption report: Quantity used, cost
- Monthly inventory summary: Stock levels, reorder status
- Wastage report: Items wasted, reason, cost
- Supplier performance: On-time delivery, quality issues
- Inventory valuation: Cost of inventory on hand (FIFO method)

5.4 INTEGRATIONS
- Pharmacy system: Auto-integration for medication dispensing
- Finance system: Cost tracking and budget reporting
- Lab system: Lab material consumption tracking
- Electronic health record: Link inventory to patient encounters

11. RISKS & MITIGATION
================================================================================

Risk: Barcode scanning failures or scanning errors
Mitigation: Manual quantity verification, batch error reports, operator training

Risk: Inventory count discrepancies at month-end close
Mitigation: Weekly spot checks, automated variance alerts, investigation protocol

Risk: Expiration date tracking failures
Mitigation: Automated alerts, manual verification, quarantine of near-expiry items

Risk: Controlled substance tracking compliance
Mitigation: Daily audit logs, monthly count verification, DEA reporting automation
