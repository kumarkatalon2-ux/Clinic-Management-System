OBJECTIVE
---------
Design and implement comprehensive EMR system serving as central repository for
all patient clinical data, medical history, encounter records, and clinical
documentation.

BUSINESS LOGIC
--------------
EMR Purpose:
EMR is the digital equivalent of patient medical chart - a complete, persistent
record of all clinical interactions with patient including:
- Patient demographics
- Medical history
- Current problems/diagnoses
- Medications
- Allergies/adverse reactions
- Vital signs
- Clinical encounters
- Test results
- Imaging reports
- Doctor notes
- Treatment plans

Key EMR Principles:

1. ACCURACY & COMPLETENESS
   - All clinical data must be accurate and complete
   - Missing data is critical (allergies, medications)
   - Corrections tracked (amendments, not deletions)
   - HIPAA requires complete medical record retention (6+ years)

2. ACCESSIBILITY
   - Authorized clinicians can access instantly
   - Quick lookup of critical information
   - Search functionality for finding specific entries
   - Historical view (what was documented when)

3. INTEGRITY & LEGALITY
   - Medical-legal record (legally admissible)
   - Audit trail (who accessed, when, why)
   - Signatures/approvals documented
   - Cannot be altered after certain period

4. CONTINUITY OF CARE
   - Complete history from first visit
   - Allows next doctor to understand patient's history
   - Supports informed clinical decisions
   - Prevents duplicate tests/prescriptions

5. COMPLIANCE
   - HIPAA-compliant data storage
   - GDPR right to access/portability
   - State privacy laws
   - Medical board regulations

EMR vs EHR vs PHR:

EMR (Electronic Medical Record):
- Clinic's internal system
- Single clinic owns the data
- Not transferable to other clinics
- Clinic's patient chart

EHR (Electronic Health Record):
- Interoperable across organizations
- Patient's complete health record across all providers
- Future goal (Phase 2+)

PHR (Personal Health Record):
- Patient-owned health record
- Patient uploads/manages data
- Shares with providers

For this system: Implementing EMR (single clinic scope)

EMR DATA STRUCTURE
------------------
Hierarchical organization:

Patient (top level)
  └─ Demographics (name, DOB, contact, insurance)
     │
     ├─ Medical History (past illnesses, surgeries, family history)
     │
     ├─ Problem List (active diagnoses, chronic conditions)
     │
     ├─ Medication List (current medications, past medications, allergies)
     │
     ├─ Vital Signs (historical blood pressure, weight, temperature, etc.)
     │
     └─ Clinical Encounters (one per visit)
          │
          ├─ Chief Complaint (why patient came in)
          ├─ History (symptoms, context)
          ├─ Physical Examination (doctor's observations)
          ├─ Assessment (doctor's diagnosis)
          ├─ Plan (treatment plan, prescriptions)
          ├─ Procedures (done during visit)
          └─ Follow-up (next steps)

CORE EMR FEATURES
-----------------
1. PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS
   ├─ Basic info: Name, DOB, gender, contact
   ├─ Insurance: Policy number, coverage details
   ├─ Emergency contacts
   ├─ Allergies & adverse reactions (CRITICAL)
   ├─ Blood type
   ├─ Preferred pharmacy
   └─ Language preference

2. PROBLEM LIST (Active Diagnoses)
   ├─ ICD-10 code
   ├─ Problem description
   ├─ Onset date
   ├─ Status (active, resolved, inactive)
   ├─ Severity
   └─ Notes

3. MEDICATION LIST
   ├─ Current medications
   │  ├─ Drug name
   │  ├─ Dosage & frequency
   │  ├─ Prescriber
   │  ├─ Start date
   │  └─ Active status
   │
   ├─ Past medications (historical)
   │  └─ When stopped & why
   │
   └─ Allergy & adverse reaction tracking
      ├─ Substance name
      ├─ Reaction type (rash, anaphylaxis, etc.)
      ├─ Severity
      └─ Date identified

4. VITAL SIGNS TRACKING
   ├─ Blood pressure (systolic/diastolic)
   ├─ Heart rate (beats per minute)
   ├─ Respiratory rate (breaths per minute)
   ├─ Temperature (Celsius/Fahrenheit)
   ├─ Weight (kg/lbs)
   ├─ Height (cm/inches)
   ├─ BMI (calculated)
   ├─ Oxygen saturation (SpO2)
   └─ Each with timestamp & recording clinician

5. CLINICAL ENCOUNTER DOCUMENTATION
   ├─ Encounter type (office visit, telemedicine, follow-up, etc.)
   ├─ Date/time
   ├─ Clinician
   ├─ Chief complaint (why patient came)
   ├─ History of present illness
   ├─ Physical examination findings
   ├─ Assessment (diagnosis)
   ├─ Plan (treatment, prescriptions, tests ordered)
   ├─ Procedures performed
   ├─ Medications prescribed
   ├─ Tests ordered
   ├─ Follow-up scheduled
   └─ Status (draft, completed, signed)

6. TEST RESULTS & IMAGING
   ├─ Lab orders & results
   ├─ Imaging reports
   ├─ Pathology results
   ├─ Reference ranges
   ├─ Abnormal value flagging
   └─ Trending (compare to previous)

7. DOCUMENTS & ATTACHMENTS
   ├─ Scanned documents
   ├─ Insurance cards
   ├─ Outside medical records
   ├─ Previous clinic records
   └─ Patient-provided documents

EMR WORKFLOW
------------
New Patient Visit:

1. REGISTRATION
   - Patient checks in
   - Demographics collected/updated
   - Insurance verified
   - Allergies confirmed

2. VITAL SIGNS
   - Nurse records vital signs
   - Alerts if abnormal (BP too high, etc.)
   - Added to vital signs history

3. CLINICAL ENCOUNTER
   - Doctor reviews chief complaint
   - Reviews medical history
   - Checks allergy list
   - Checks current medications
   - Performs examination
   - Documents findings

4. ASSESSMENT & PLAN
   - Doctor enters diagnosis (ICD-10 code)
   - Doctor enters plan (treatment, medication, tests)
   - Orders prescriptions
   - Orders tests/imaging
   - Schedules follow-up

5. DOCUMENTATION
   - Encounter saved (draft)
   - Doctor reviews and signs (legal signature)
   - Encounter locked (cannot edit after signing)
   - Patient can access (patient portal)

6. FOLLOW-UP
   - Test results come back, added to EMR
   - Follow-up appointment scheduled
   - Referral sent to specialist
   - Summary sent to patient

CLINICAL DECISION SUPPORT
-------------------------
EMR should support clinical decisions:

1. ALLERGY CHECKING
   - Doctor prescribes medication
   - System checks: Patient allergic to this? Interaction?
   - Alert: "PATIENT ALLERGIC TO PENICILLIN"
   - Doctor acknowledges or cancels prescription

2. DRUG INTERACTION CHECKING
   - Doctor adds medication
   - System checks interactions with current medications
   - Alert if serious interactions found
   - Example: "WARFARIN + ASPIRIN = BLEEDING RISK"

3. LAB VALUE NORMALIZATION
   - Lab result comes in
   - Compare to reference range
   - Flag if abnormal
   - Trend if historical data available
   - Example: "Glucose 285 (HIGH, normal 70-100)"

4. PROBLEM-MEDICATION LINKING
   - Suggest medications for diagnosed problem
   - Example: "Patient has diabetes, consider metformin"
   - Not automatic, but helpful prompt

5. DUPLICATE PREVENTION
   - Check: Already ordered this test recently?
   - Check: Already documented this diagnosis?
   - Prevent unnecessary tests/duplicate work

SECURITY & COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS
-----------------------------------
HIPAA Requirements:

1. Access Control
   - Only authorized clinicians access patient data
   - Role-based (doctor sees more than receptionist)
   - Audit trail of all access
   - Alert on suspicious access patterns

2. Data Encryption
   - All patient data encrypted at rest (AES-256)
   - All transmission encrypted (TLS 1.3)
   - PII fields encrypted (name, SSN, medical record number)

3. Audit Logging
   - Log: Who accessed what patient record
   - Log: When they accessed it
   - Log: What they viewed/edited
   - Retention: 6 years minimum
   - Cannot be altered after creation

4. Data Integrity
   - No unauthorized modification
   - Medical records legally binding
   - Amendment trail (what was changed, when, by who)
   - Cannot delete after certain period

5. Backup & Disaster Recovery
   - Daily backups of all EMR data
   - Tested restore capability
   - Recovery time objective (RTO): 1 hour
   - Recovery point objective (RPO): 1 hour

GDPR Requirements:

1. Data Subject Access
   - Patient can request their data
   - System generates export (PDF, JSON)
   - Delivered within 30 days

2. Right to Erasure
   - Patient can request deletion
   - Soft delete from production
   - Hard delete from backups after retention period
   - Exception: Legal holds, regulatory requirements

3. Data Portability
   - Patient data exported in standard format
   - Can be transferred to another clinic
