OBJECTIVE
---------
Implement comprehensive medical history tracking system capturing patient's
complete health timeline including past illnesses, surgeries, hospitalizations,
family history, and social history.

BUSINESS LOGIC
--------------
Medical History Purpose:

Medical history answers the question: "What health events has this patient
experienced in their lifetime?"

Why It Matters:
1. CLINICAL DECISION MAKING
   - Doctor knows patient's surgical history
   - Doctor knows previous surgeries → knows complications
   - Doctor knows allergies to medications
   - Doctor knows chronic conditions

2. PATTERN RECOGNITION
   - Recurrent infections → might indicate immune problem
   - Multiple surgeries → patient may be high-risk
   - Diabetes + heart disease → metabolic syndrome pattern
   - Family history of cancer → screening needed

3. PREVENTIVE CARE
   - Family history of heart disease → aggressive BP management
   - Smoking history → lung cancer screening needed
   - Age 50+ → colonoscopy needed
   - Blood clot history → anticoagulation needed

4. MEDICATION SAFETY
   - Previous adverse drug reaction → avoid that drug
   - Kidney disease → avoid nephrotoxic drugs
   - Liver disease → avoid hepatotoxic drugs
   - Pregnancy → avoid teratogenic drugs

MEDICAL HISTORY COMPONENTS
--------------------------
1. PAST MEDICAL HISTORY (PMHx)
   ├─ Previous diagnoses (resolved or chronic)
   ├─ Previous hospitalizations
   ├─ Previous surgeries
   ├─ Previous injuries/trauma
   └─ Timeline of major health events

2. SURGICAL HISTORY
   ├─ Surgery name (appendectomy, hysterectomy, etc.)
   ├─ Date performed
   ├─ Hospital/clinic where performed
   ├─ Surgeon name
   ├─ Indications (why was it done)
   ├─ Complications (if any)
   └─ Outcome

3. HOSPITALIZATION HISTORY
   ├─ Hospital name
   ├─ Admission date
   ├─ Discharge date
   ├─ Reason for admission
   ├─ Procedures performed
   ├─ Outcome
   └─ Discharge summary

4. INJURY/TRAUMA HISTORY
   ├─ Type of injury (car accident, fall, sports injury)
   ├─ Date
   ├─ Resulting injuries
   ├─ Treatment received
   ├─ Residual effects (permanent disability)
   └─ Follow-up care needed

5. CHRONIC CONDITIONS
   ├─ Condition (diabetes, hypertension, COPD, etc.)
   ├─ Date diagnosed
   ├─ Severity
   ├─ Current status (controlled, poorly controlled)
   ├─ Medications for this condition
   └─ Complications related to this condition

6. FAMILY HISTORY
   ├─ Father
   │  ├─ Alive (Y/N), age at death (if deceased)
   │  ├─ Health conditions (diabetes, heart disease, etc.)
   │  └─ Cause of death (if deceased)
   │
   ├─ Mother
   │  └─ Same as father
   │
   ├─ Siblings
   │  ├─ Number of siblings
   │  ├─ Each sibling: conditions, outcomes
   │  └─ Age/status
   │
   ├─ Children
   │  └─ Any genetic conditions inherited?
   │
   └─ Relatives with genetic conditions
      ├─ Cancer (type, age at diagnosis)
      ├─ Heart disease (age at event)
      ├─ Diabetes (type, age at diagnosis)
      └─ Genetic disorders (sickle cell, cystic fibrosis, etc.)

7. SOCIAL HISTORY
   ├─ SMOKING
   │  ├─ Never smoker
   │  ├─ Former smoker (quit when, how many years)
   │  ├─ Current smoker (how many cigarettes/day, start age)
   │  └─ Pack-years (cigarettes per day × years)
   │
   ├─ ALCOHOL
   │  ├─ Non-drinker
   │  ├─ Social drinker
   │  ├─ Heavy drinker (how many drinks/week)
   │  ├─ Substance abuse history (drugs, alcohol)
   │  └─ Recovery status (if applicable)
   │
   ├─ EXERCISE
   │  ├─ Sedentary (no exercise)
   │  ├─ Light (exercise <3x/week)
   │  ├─ Moderate (3-5x/week)
   │  └─ Vigorous (>5x/week)
   │
   ├─ OCCUPATION
   │  ├─ Current job
   │  ├─ Occupational exposures (chemicals, dust, etc.)
   │  ├─ Previous jobs with exposures
   │  └─ Disability status
   │
   ├─ LIVING SITUATION
   │  ├─ Lives alone / with family / in facility
   │  ├─ Housing status (owned, rented, homeless)
   │  ├─ Access to transportation
   │  └─ Access to pharmacy
   │
   ├─ EDUCATION LEVEL
   │  └─ None, high school, college, graduate
   │
   └─ SOCIAL SUPPORT
      ├─ Family support system
      ├─ Financial stress
      └─ Food security (access to food)

8. IMMUNIZATION HISTORY
   ├─ Vaccine name
   ├─ Date administered
   ├─ Dose number (if multi-dose series)
   ├─ Route (IV, IM, oral, intranasal)
   ├─ Clinic/provider who administered
   └─ Reactions (if any)

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY WORKFLOW
-----------------------------
New Patient Intake:

1. HISTORY COLLECTION
   - Patient arrives for first visit
   - Medical history form provided (paper or digital)
   - Patient completes (or staff interviews)
   - Covers: Past illnesses, surgeries, hospitalizations, medications, allergies

2. CLINICAL REVIEW
   - Doctor reviews history
   - Asks clarifying questions
   - Confirms accuracy
   - Updates if needed

3. DOCUMENTATION
   - Entered into EMR
   - Structured data (dropdown menus, not free text)
   - Free text notes for additional context
   - Audit trail recorded

4. PROBLEM LIST CREATION
   - Extracted from history
   - Active problems marked as "active"
   - Resolved problems marked as "resolved"
   - Used as baseline for ongoing care

CHRONIC CONDITION MANAGEMENT
----------------------------
Chronic Condition Entry:

POST /patients/{patientId}/medical-history/conditions
{
  "conditionName": "Type 2 Diabetes",
  "icdCode": "E11.9",  // ICD-10 code
  "dateOfDiagnosis": "2010-03-15",
  "severity": "moderate",  // mild, moderate, severe
  "status": "active",  // active, controlled, poorly-controlled, resolved
  "medications": ["metformin", "lisinopril"],
  "complications": ["diabetic neuropathy", "hypertension"],
  "notes": "Diagnosed 15 years ago, poorly controlled recently"
}

Chronic conditions used for:
- Problem list (displayed in EMR)
- Medication safety checking (avoid drug interactions)
- Screening recommendations (diabetes → annual eye exam)
- Risk stratification (multiple chronic conditions = higher risk)

FAMILY HISTORY TRACKING
----------------------
Family History Structure:

Family Member Relationship → Medical Conditions → Outcomes

Example:
- Father: Died at 65 from MI (myocardial infarction)
- Mother: Hypertension, age 72
- Brother: Type 2 diabetes
- Sister: Healthy

Clinical Implications:
- Patient (likely age ~45): High risk for early MI
- Patient's family should be screened

Family History Entry:

POST /patients/{patientId}/family-history
{
  "relationship": "father",  // father, mother, sibling, child, grandparent
  "name": "John Senior",
  "dateOfBirth": "1935-06-15",
  "isAlive": false,
  "ageAtDeath": 65,
  "causeOfDeath": "Myocardial infarction",
  "medicalConditions": [
    {
      "condition": "Hypertension",
      "ageAtOnset": 45
    },
    {
      "condition": "Myocardial infarction",
      "ageAtOnset": 65
    }
  ]
}

Family Health Summary:
GET /patients/{patientId}/family-history/summary

Returns risk assessment:
- Cardiovascular disease: HIGH RISK (multiple family members)
- Diabetes: MODERATE RISK (one sibling)
- Cancer: LOW RISK (none in family)

SOCIAL HISTORY TRACKING
-----------------------
Smoking History Example:

{
  "smokingStatus": "former",
  "startAge": 18,
  "quitAge": 35,
  "cigarettesPerDay": 20,  // historical
  "packYears": 17,  // (cigarettes per day / 20) × years smoking = (20/20) × 17
  "notes": "Quit 10 years ago, using nicotine patches"
}

Why It Matters:
- Smoking → COPD, lung cancer, heart disease screening
- Former smoker → Still needs more screening than never-smoker
- Alcohol use → Liver disease screening, medication interactions
- Drug history → Addiction medicine, pain management considerations

CLINICAL DECISION SUPPORT
-------------------------
Based on medical history, system provides:

1. PREVENTIVE SCREENING ALERTS
   Medical History → Recommended Screening
   
   - Age 50+ → Colonoscopy recommended
   - Smoking history → Lung cancer screening (LDCT)
   - Hypertension → Annual cardiac exam
   - Diabetes → Annual eye exam, kidney function test
   - Family history of breast cancer → Earlier mammography

2. MEDICATION SAFETY ALERTS
   Medical History → Drug Contraindications
   
   - Kidney disease → Avoid nephrotoxic drugs
   - Liver disease → Avoid hepatotoxic drugs
   - History of GI bleed → Avoid NSAIDs
   - Diabetes → Caution with certain antibiotics
   - Asthma history → Avoid beta-blockers

3. RISK STRATIFICATION
   Medical History → Risk Score
   
   High Risk Factors:
   - Age > 65
   - Multiple chronic conditions
   - Recent hospitalization
   - Multiple emergency visits
   - Non-adherence to medications
   
   Risk Score used for:
   - Care coordination (high-risk patients need more support)
   - Preventive interventions (high-risk get priority)
   - Hospital readmission prevention programs
